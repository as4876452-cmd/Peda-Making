Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

❖

Find immediate answers to questions regarding our ingredients, freshness, storage, and shipping.

How fresh is the peda when delivered?

We never deliver pre-stored sweets. Every order placed is prepared fresh on the day of dispatch using morning farm milk, then vacuum-sealed to preserve that authentic warm, tender texture for up to 15-20 days.

What ingredients do you use?

Our pedas contain 100% pure condensed milk solids (khoya/mawa) from grass-fed cows, pure desi ghee, natural unrefined sugar (bura), genuine Kashmiri saffron threads (kesar), stone-ground green cardamom, and California almonds/pistachios.

Do you provide custom festival & wedding gift packaging?

Yes! We offer regal gold-embossed gift boxes, brass hampers, customized greeting sleeves, and personalized corporate branding for weddings, Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, and celebratory events.

Can I place bulk orders and what are the delivery timelines?

Yes, we fulfill orders from single gift boxes to 500+ Kg corporate and wedding consignments. Standard orders ship within 24 hours, while custom bulk hampers require 2–3 days advance intimation. We ship across India via express temperature-safe courier.

How should the peda be stored?

Store the peda in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight in an airtight container. During hot summer months, refrigeration is recommended to maintain freshness. For optimal taste, bring to room temperature 15 minutes before serving.
