How We Make Our Peda

An artisanal 8-step journey converting farm-fresh milk into royal sweet perfection.

The Culinary Alchemy

From Fresh Milk to Perfect Peda

True peda is an art of patience. Unlike factory confections, authentic Mathura peda cannot be rushed. It requires precise temperature regulation, continuous manual stirring with heavy ladles, and the discerning intuition of master sweet makers.

4-5 Hours Slow Simmer
Traditional Copper Kadhais
Step-by-Step Heritage

Our Master 8-Step Craftsmanship

❖

Follow each stage of our time-honored recipe that turns pure milk into heavenly dessert.

1

1. Selecting Fresh Milk

We procure unadulterated whole milk every morning from trusted local dairy farmers. Each batch undergoes rigorous testing for fat content, purity, and sweetness.

100% Gir Cow Whole Milk
2

2. Preparing Khoya

Fresh milk is brought to a boil in wide, thick-bottomed vessels. It is steadily reduced over high heat while being continuously scraped from the sides.

High-Heat Reduction
3

3. Slow Cooking

As the milk solids thicken into mawa (khoya), the flame is lowered. The mawa is gently roasted in pure desi ghee to achieve a signature golden-brown color and roasted aroma.

Rich Caramelization
4

4. Adding Sugar & Flavors

Fine bura (natural unrefined cane sugar), hand-crushed green cardamom powder, and steeped Kashmiri saffron extracts are gently folded into the warm mixture.

Kashmiri Saffron & Cardamom
5

5. Shaping the Peda

Once the peda dough reaches the perfect consistency and temperature, master artisans hand-roll equal portions and press them using heritage floral brass stamps.

Traditional Brass Stamping
6

6. Garnishing

Each peda is crowned with slivered California almonds, vibrant green pistachios, and saffron threads for an exquisite royal finish and delightful crunch.

Premium Dry Fruit Toppings
7

7. Quality Checking

Every completed batch undergoes stringent inspection for texture, moisture equilibrium, aroma, and taste consistency by senior food safety supervisors.

Multi-Point Quality Audit
8

8. Premium Packaging

Packaged in airtight, gold-embossed food-grade luxury gift boxes that lock in freshness, prevent moisture ingress, and ensure pristine arrival at your door.

Vacuum Fresh Sealed
