Our Heritage

“Tradition in Every Bite, Purity in Every Piece”

Founded in the sacred land celebrated for its eternal love and sweet craftsmanship, Shree Radha Peda was established with a singular devotion: to preserve the authentic taste of pure Indian peda without shortcuts or artificial adulterants.

We continue to follow the slow-reduction methods practiced by royal Halwais, taking pure whole milk and simmering it patiently until it turns into golden, aromatic mawa.
