Our Sweet Story

Preserving authentic Indian confectionery traditions with uncompromised purity and modern hygiene.

100% Pure Cow Milk Khoya with No Preservatives
Rooted in Brij Bhoomi

Where Devotion Meets Confectionery Craft

Founded in Mathura, the historical epicenter of authentic Indian peda making, Shree Radha Peda began with a single vision: to bring the authentic, melt-in-the-mouth taste of traditional peda to sweet lovers across the nation.

In a world overrun by artificial flavors and mass-produced shortcuts, we stay loyal to the traditional art of slow cooking pure whole milk in large kadhais. Every batch requires hours of patient stirring over an open flame until the milk condenses into rich, caramelized khoya.

While our recipes remain deeply traditional, our kitchen operates with modern, sterile standards. We combine old-world recipes with advanced temperature controls and automated hermetic packaging to ensure absolute hygiene.

Our Mission

To serve authentic, unadulterated traditional Indian peda that revives nostalgic flavors while setting the benchmark for food safety and artisan quality.

Pure Ingredients

We strictly refuse artificial thickeners, starch (maida), chemical preservatives, or synthetic food colors. Only fresh milk, natural saffron, and real cardamom.

Honest Craftsmanship

Our master halwais carry over two decades of experience. Each peda is shaped with reverent care and adorned with royal floral embossments.
